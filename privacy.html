Privacy notice (pre-launch)

Last updated: 21 November 2026. This is an initial pre-launch notice — have it reviewed before commercial launch.

Information collected

  • Beta registration (Google Forms): email (required, verified by Google), use case, and expected monthly token usage (required). Name, company, current AI provider, country/region, and additional comments are optional. Signup timestamp and campaign/source parameters may also be recorded.
  • Site analytics (only with your consent): pages viewed, referring campaign parameters (UTM), approximate device type, and interaction events such as CTA clicks. We do not send names, email addresses, form contents, API keys, passwords, or other personal data to analytics.

Why information is collected

To validate developer demand, size capacity, plan free-tier economics and pricing, notify you about the beta, and understand which parts of the site are useful.

Email communication

Beta communication only when you consent (consent is captured in the Google Form). No sale of personal data. No advertising trackers.

Analytics and cookies

Analytics is off by default. Google Analytics 4 is loaded only after you select Accept in the cookie banner. If you select Reject, no Google Analytics script is loaded and no analytics events are stored by this site.

  • Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC) — when enabled and accepted, sets first-party cookies such as _ga and _ga_* to distinguish browsers and measure site usage. IP anonymization is enabled, Google Signals and advertising personalization are disabled, and advertising consent remains denied.
  • Consent storage — your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (v26_analytics_consent_v1). Campaign attribution parameters (UTM/ref) are also stored locally so registrations can be attributed in the beta form.
  • No local event log before consent — the first-party funnel log is only written after you accept.

Your current analytics choice: Not set. at any time. Rejecting after accepting stops further analytics and clears Google Analytics cookies this site can access.

Data deletion / contact process

Email hello@v26-inference.com with subject “Delete my data” or “Unsubscribe”. We will confirm within 30 days.

Third parties

  • Google Forms / Google Sheets — beta registration is collected and stored in Google's systems under Google's privacy policy. The form opens in a new tab.
  • Google Analytics 4 — optional, consent-gated analytics as described above.
  • GitHub Pages — the site is served as static files by GitHub Pages, which processes standard request logs (including IP address) to deliver the site.
  • Font and asset delivery — web fonts are requested from Google Fonts and jsDelivr; these providers receive the requesting IP address and user agent as part of normal content delivery.

Your choices

You can accept or reject analytics at any time using the cookie banner or the “Cookie settings” control in the footer. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser. Rejecting analytics does not prevent you from using the site or registering for the beta.
