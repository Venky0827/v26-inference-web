Privacy notice (pre-launch)

Last updated: 2026. This is an initial pre-launch notice — have it reviewed before commercial launch.

What we collect

  • Email, name/company if provided, use case, expected usage, current providers, comments, referral code.
  • Signup timestamp, UTM/source, device type, country/region where legally appropriate.
  • Local-only analytics events until a privacy-conscious provider is configured (see analytics.js).

Why

To validate demand, size GPU capacity, plan free-tier economics and pricing, and notify you about the beta — the Phase 1 business requirement (§63).

How we use it

Beta communication only when you consent (consent is captured in the Google Form). No sale of personal data. No advertising trackers in Phase 1.

Deletion & unsubscribe

Email hello@v26-inference.com with subject “Delete my data” or “Unsubscribe”. We will confirm within 30 days.

Cookies / tracking

Local storage for waitlist placeholder + cookie-notice flag. No third-party cookies unless you configure Plausible/PostHog with consent.

Third parties

Registration runs on Google Forms (responses stored in Google's systems under Google's privacy policy — link your form's privacy notice). Hosting (Cloudflare/Vercel), and later: managed PostgreSQL, transactional email (Resend/Postmark/SES), error monitoring (Sentry). Listed in deployment docs when live.
